About Farm Direct

Bringing farmers and customers closer together.

🌾 Our Mission

Farm Direct was created to remove middlemen from the farming supply chain, so farmers earn fair prices and customers get fresher produce at lower costs.

🧑‍🌾 Who We Are

We are a small college project team passionate about supporting local agriculture using simple, accessible web technology.

🌍 Our Vision

We want to build a future where every local farmer has direct access to customers in their community, without unnecessary layers in between.

❤️ Our Values

Honesty, freshness, fair pricing, and strong support for local farming communities guide everything we do.

How Farm Direct Works

1. Farmers List Products

Local farmers register on our platform and list their fresh produce with prices.

2. Customers Browse & Order

Customers search, filter, and add products to their cart directly from the site.

3. Direct Delivery

Orders are delivered straight from the farm to the customer's doorstep.
